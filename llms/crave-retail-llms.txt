# Crave Retail

> Crave Retail, Inc. builds in-store retail technology that connects shoppers, store associates, and product data across physical touchpoints — smart fitting rooms, sales-floor assistants, and mobile. Reported scale: 3,000+ live touchpoints, 27M+ shopper sessions, across 10+ countries. Techstars-backed. No public developer API or documentation is published at this time.

## Products
- [Crave Engage](https://craveretail.com/engage.html): Customer-facing digital shopping assistants in fitting rooms, on the sales floor, and on mobile.
- [Crave Assist](https://craveretail.com/assist.html): Store-associate tools — floor visibility, service routing, and item lookup.
- [Smart Fitting Rooms](https://craveretail.com/fitting-rooms.html): RFID-based connected fitting-room technology (the company's original product).
